# Pronunciation reference

US broad IPA is stored in content/word-reference.tsv and in every vocabulary card. Existing pronunciation fields were retained where present. The official CMU Pronouncing Dictionary was used to cross-check covered headwords: https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict . Its ARPAbet notation was consulted rather than copied into the cards. Remaining specialist headwords have explicit IPA entries in the local reference.

The card layout follows the eleven October 15 reference images supplied by the owner: portrait 660×1434, essay 1434×660. Original source archives stay unchanged.
